The apps

Meet the flock.

Eight apps for recovery, calm, podcasting, money and learning. Each one does a single job well and treats the people who use it with respect.

    A steady place in the storm.

    No. 01Wellbeing

    A private, offline-first companion for anyone choosing sobriety. A live counter celebrates every milestone, a quick daily check-in tracks mood and cravings, and an emergency toolkit is there the moment a craving hits.

    Field marks
    No account · No data collection · Works offline

    Find your frequency.

    No. 02Wellbeing

How we build

Four rules we build by.

  1. I

    Private by design

    Anchor works offline, with no account and no data collection. Avocadoge and Perch never ask you to sign up either.

    Seen in

  2. II

    You hold the keys

    Avocadoge creates your keys on your iPhone and they never leave it. Avocadough is a remote for a wallet you already own. Boostz sends support straight to the host.

    Seen in

  3. III

    Open protocols

    Effem is built on the AT Protocol and the Podcast Index, and Avocadough speaks Nostr Wallet Connect. We build on networks no single company controls, us included.

    Seen in

  4. IV

    Native to the core

    Our iPhone apps are written in Swift and SwiftUI and kept current with each new iOS, so they feel at home on your iPhone instead of merely running on it.

    Seen in

Open source

The building blocks are yours, too.

The Swift packages underneath our apps are open source. Use them, fork them, send a fix.

GitHub Tangled
  • AT Protocol CoreATProtocol The networking layer for AT Protocol apps: OAuth, sessions, identity resolution and repo records. tangled.org
  • AT Protocol EffemKit The client for Effem’s AppView: subscriptions, comments, bookmarks, lists and profiles. tangled.org
  • AT Protocol · Lightning AvocadoughKit Turns an AT Protocol identity into a Bitcoin payment address, from handle to invoice. tangled.org
  • Podcasting 2.0 PodcastIndexKit A client for the Podcast Index API: search, podcasts, episodes, categories and value-for-value. github.com
  • Nostr CoreNostr Nostr protocol primitives in Swift: keys, events and signing. github.com
  • Nostr NostrKit Relay management, caching and Nostr Wallet Connect for Apple platforms. github.com
  • Cashu CashuKit Cashu ecash for Apple platforms, built on CoreCashu. Currently in beta. github.com
  • Dogecoin DogecoinKit A pure Swift SDK for SPV Dogecoin wallets: HD keys, transactions and peer-to-peer sync. github.com
  • Stacks StacksKit A native Swift take on the Stacks.js core: keys, transactions, Clarity values and stacking. github.com
  • Education CoreLittleManComputer The Little Man Computer as a Swift package: machine, assembler and a runner that plays nicely with SwiftUI. github.com
  • Networking NetworkingKit Typed endpoints on top of URLSession and Swift concurrency. github.com
  • Keychain Vault Save, read and delete strings in the iOS and macOS keychain. github.com
